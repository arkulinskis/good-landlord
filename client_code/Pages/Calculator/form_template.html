Rules of thumb

Quick checks investors use before looking closer. None of them decides for you.

Cap rate

Rent minus costs over a year, divided by the price (before the mortgage). Around 5% or more is usually healthy.

Debt cover

Rent minus costs, divided by the mortgage. Lenders like 1.2 or more.

Break-even rent

The rent that covers the mortgage and the costs, with nothing left over.

The 1% rule

Monthly rent as a share of the price. 1% is the old rule; in most cities today 0.5% to 0.7% is normal.
