Quick ratios

Rules of thumb investors use. None of them tells the whole story.

Cap rate

Yearly rent minus costs (no mortgage), over the value. 4 to 6% is common in cities.

Cash-on-cash

A year of cash flow over the money you put in.

Rent to value

Monthly rent over the value. The old "1% rule" is rare today.
