How it works

Ten minutes to set up, then a few taps a month.

  1. Add your rentals

    Purchase price, mortgage, today's value. Good Landlord works out payments, interest and equity for you.

  2. Record money in and out

    Mark rent paid in one tap; file each expense under a tax category so the year-end summary writes itself.

  3. Send each tenant their link

    They write and ask for repairs there. You move requests along; every date is on record for both of you.

Categories that follow the rental tax forms (T776, Schedule E), with mortgage interest worked out for you.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Good Landlord is free to use, with no ads.

Does my tenant need to sign up?

No. You send them a private link. Whoever has the link can read and write in that tenancy's record, so send it only to your tenant. You can make a new link at any time, which stops the old one.

Can anyone delete or change a message?

No. There's no edit or delete button for anyone, landlord included. Each entry is sealed with a SHA-256 fingerprint of its text, its time and the entry before it, so a change made behind the scenes would show up as a broken seal. Both sides can download the whole record.

Is this tax advice?

No. It sorts what you record into the usual rental-income lines so you (or your accountant) can fill the forms faster. Rules differ by country and situation.

What does my tenant see?

Their own lease (rent, due day, deposit), the messages and requests, the rent you recorded as received, and your response badges. Never your other properties, costs or numbers.
